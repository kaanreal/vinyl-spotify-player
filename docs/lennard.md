# Lennard – Development Documentation

## Role

Mechanical Design & Prototyping

Responsibilities:

- 3D modelling
- Prototype development
- Mechanical design support
- Assembly preparation
- Design optimization and testing

---

## Personal Contribution

My main responsibility during the project was supporting the mechanical development of the Vinyl Spotify Player.

I worked closely with Kaan on the design and development of the enclosure and internal component layout. Throughout the project, I helped create, test, and improve multiple 3D models to ensure that all hardware components could fit correctly inside the final design.

To reduce unnecessary material costs, I also created and tested small prototypes before producing larger prints.

---

## Development Timeline

### 9 April 2026

Before the official project start, Kaan, Marc, and I met to discuss possible project ideas. We collected several concepts and evaluated their feasibility.

After comparing different options, we decided to develop a Spotify-enabled vinyl player. We then began researching the required hardware and discussing the overall design.

---

### 14 April 2026

Official project start.

The project structure was established and initial planning began.

---

### 16 April 2026

The team started sourcing and purchasing components.

This process took several days because we wanted to stay within budget while still selecting suitable hardware. Kaan eventually found the most cost-effective components for the project.

---

### 23 April 2026

Most hardware had already been selected.

Since some component dimensions were still unknown, the main focus was on organizing responsibilities and planning future development steps.

---

### 28 April 2026

The final dimensions of several components were confirmed.

This information was necessary before starting the enclosure design.

---

### 30 April 2026

I began working on the first 3D design concepts.

Initially, I attempted to use Tinkercad, but it quickly became clear that the software was not suitable for the level of precision required by the project.

I then switched to Onshape.

---

### 5 May 2026

I continued experimenting with Onshape and learning the software.

Due to account-related issues, I collaborated with Kaan using his workspace so we could continue development without delays.

---

### 12 May – 23 May 2026

This period was focused on creating and improving the main enclosure design.

Together with Kaan, I spent several hours developing the first complete models and testing different layouts.

To avoid unnecessary printing costs, I first created smaller prototype parts to verify measurements and functionality.

Several revisions were required because of scaling issues, component tolerances, and fitment problems. As a result, additional work had to be completed at home outside normal project hours.

---

### 28 May 2026

Most of the mechanical design was completed.

The enclosure was prepared for final assembly, and only a few hardware components were still missing.

---

### 9 June 2026

Final inspections and adjustments were performed.

The team reviewed the design, checked printed parts, and ensured that there was enough time remaining to address any final issues before the project deadline.

---

## Challenges

Some of the biggest challenges included:

- Learning new CAD software
- Accurate measurement of hardware components
- Scaling and fitment problems
- Limited project time
- Delays caused by missing hardware
